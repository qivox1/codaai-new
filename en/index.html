Questions & Answers

(01) How does CodaAI replace a content agency?
CodaAI combines six AI experts in one platform: editorial planning, content writing, graphic design, SEO optimization, video production, and social media management. You provide your URL, and we deliver a complete content strategy with articles, visuals, and distribution plan.
(02) What does 'orchestration by six AI experts' mean?
Like a conductor leading an orchestra, CodaAI coordinates six specialized AI modules that work together seamlessly: Strategy & Planning, Content Writing, Visual Design, SEO & GEO, Video Production, and Distribution. Each 'expert' handles their specialty while CodaAI ensures perfect harmony.
(03) How much can I actually save?
Traditional content agencies charge €2,000+ per blog article, plus costs for images, videos, and coordination. With CodaAI, you can save up to 88% compared to agency costs – from as little as €490*/month with an annual licence. Check our pricing section to see how much you can save based on your content volume.
(04) How does it work? What do I need to get started?
Simply provide your website URL. CodaAI analyzes your brand, industry, and target audience, then generates a complete editorial plan, SEO-optimized articles, professional graphics, videos, and a publication strategy. No lengthy briefings, no coordination meetings.
(05) What makes CodaAI different from other AI writing tools?
Most AI tools focus on single tasks like writing or image generation. CodaAI is a complete content marketing platform – we don't just create content, we plan, produce, optimize, and distribute. We're the agency you don't need anymore.
(06) Is CodaAI suitable for my industry?
CodaAI works for any B2B or B2C business that needs content marketing. Our AI adapts to your industry's terminology, tone, and audience expectations. From tech startups to traditional enterprises – we speak your language.
(07) What contract terms are available?
The minimum contract term is one quarter (3 months). With an annual licence, you save 20% compared to quarterly billing – ideal for content marketing and SEO initiatives that deliver results over time. Cancellation is possible at the end of the chosen billing period (quarterly or annually).
(08) Can I change my plan or article volume?
Absolutely! You can adjust your article volume up or down at any time. Changes take effect in the next billing period. Whether you need 4 articles or 12 per month – scale your content production to match your business needs.
(09) What happens after I cancel?
If you cancel, your subscription ends at the close of your current billing period (quarterly or annual). You retain full rights to all content created during your subscription. There are no hidden fees and no cancellation charges.
(10) Why should I plan for at least 3–6 months?
Content marketing for search engine and AI visibility is a long-term investment. Search engines first need to crawl and index new content – depending on your site and competition, this typically takes 2–8 weeks. After that, algorithms need further weeks or months to evaluate relevance, authority, and engagement before improving rankings. Generative AI models (e.g. ChatGPT, Perplexity) also don't update their knowledge base daily – new content needs to be indexed and cited widely before it appears there. Our recommendation: plan for at least 6–12 months to achieve sustained, measurable visibility gains. The 3-month minimum term is the absolute starting point – clients who publish consistently over a full year consistently see the greatest results.
