Digital PR & Content Distribution is the work of getting third parties to write about a brand: trade publications, industry media and specialist blogs, guest articles and expert quotes, placements in comparison and "best of" articles. The guiding question is: are you written about in recognised publications? For AI systems, such mentions are the evidence they draw on for brand-neutral questions.

## How does Digital PR work for AI visibility?

For questions about providers, comparisons and recommendations, AI systems predominantly cite third-party sites. Analyses — such as the study on AI citations reported by Search Engine Land and the evaluations by georaiser on earned media — show that listicles, trade articles and product pages account for the majority of citations. A brand that appears in these sources is mentioned; a brand that is missing there is also missing from the answer. Digital PR ensures that the brand is present in exactly these sources — with a consistent description and, where possible, with a named expert.

## Why does Digital PR matter for AI visibility?

Because a brand's own website is rarely cited for brand-neutral questions. An AI system does not answer "Which providers for X are there?" with one provider's website, but with comparisons and trade articles. Anyone who only works on their own website is invisible for these questions. Digital PR is therefore the second tier of visibility: being where the AI gets its evidence.

## What does this mean for your website?

Use a [source analysis](/en/knowledge/geo-glossary/source-analysis/) to determine which domains are cited in your subject area, and work specifically on presence there: guest articles, expert quotes with name and role, placement in [listicles](/en/knowledge/geo-glossary/listicles/), data from your own surveys that journalists can cite. Make sure every mention describes your brand in the same way ([consistent brand description](/en/knowledge/geo-glossary/consistent-brand-description/)).
