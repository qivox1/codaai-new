Topical authority is the authority of a domain in a specific topic area, as it emerges from the entire content cluster. The question is not "Is there a good page on this topic?" but "Does the sum of the pages signal that this domain has mastered the topic?". A company with one page on heat pumps has no cluster; a company with pages on sizing, subsidies, operating costs, noise protection and maintenance of heat pumps has one.

## How does topical authority work?

AI systems break a question down into several sub-questions ([query fan-out](/en/knowledge/geo-glossary/query-fan-out/)) and collect the best pages for each. A domain that ranks for many of these sub-questions appears in several partial results and is considered correspondingly more often when sources are selected. At the same time, models learn during training which domain is linked to which topic — and a cluster produces more and more consistent links than a single page. Hence the rule: think in topic clusters rather than in individual keyword-optimised pages.

## Why does topical authority matter for AI visibility?

Because AI systems do not look for the best page but for reliable sources on a topic. A domain with a cluster covers the fan-out queries ([query coverage](/en/knowledge/geo-glossary/query-coverage/)), delivers a matching chunk for every sub-question and acts in the model knowledge as an entity that belongs to the topic. A single page can win for one question; a cluster wins for the topic area.

## What does this mean for your website?

Choose the two or three topic areas you want to be found in and build a cluster for each: one main page, beneath it one page or one section for every question customers ask before, during and after the decision. Link the pages to each other and use the same terms throughout. Add your own data and experience ([information gain](/en/knowledge/geo-glossary/information-gain/)), otherwise the cluster is broad but not deep.
