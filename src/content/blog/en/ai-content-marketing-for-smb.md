## The AI-Powered Mid-Market Content Strategy

### Month 1–2: Foundation

- Identify 8 buyer problems your service solves
- Create 2 pillar articles (core service areas) with AI + internal review
- Set up keyword tracking in Google Search Console

### Month 3–6: Scaling

- Add 4–6 cluster articles monthly (specific problem focus)
- Publish 2x/week on LinkedIn
- Track first rankings

### Month 6+: Dominance

- Mature keywords bring 100–300 monthly visits
- Leads from organic consistently increase
- Optimize top-performing content
