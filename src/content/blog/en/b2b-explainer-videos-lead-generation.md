## Why Video Is So Critical in the B2B Purchasing Process

The misconception about B2B video content: many mid-market companies think of YouTube channels with thousands of subscribers, elaborate production teams and expensive campaigns. That's not what this is about.

B2B explainer videos address a specific problem: many complex product features can't be explained in one sentence. An explanatory sales call takes 30–60 minutes. The video does it in 90 seconds.

What works on LinkedIn in B2B:

- **Product videos:** "How it works", explaining what your solution does
- **Testimonial videos:** Customer success stories from actual users (more credible than text reviews)
- **How-To videos:** Step-by-step guides on using your product or solving common problems
- **Onboarding videos:** Video tutorials for new customers (reduces support requests by 30–40%)

Which category works best depends on your sales cycle:

- **Short sales cycles (under 3 months):** Product and how-to videos reduce buying friction
- **Long sales cycles (6+ months):** Thought leadership videos (expert tips, industry trends) build authority

## A Complete Video Content Workflow with AI

**Week 1: Plan Topics**

From your blog article topics, identify which 3–5 are ideal for video explanation (complex processes, abstract concepts, solutions difficult to describe in text).

**Week 2: Script**

Write or have AI generate short scripts (150–200 words, ~2 minutes speaking time). AI video tools handle the rest.

**Week 3: Production**

Create videos via HeyGen, Synthesia or similar. 24-hour turnaround per video.

**Week 4: Deployment**

- Email: Insert video in relevant nurturing email campaigns
- Blog: Embed alongside article (if article explains concepts, video shows them)
- LinkedIn: Clip 15–30 second highlight, link to full video or blog
- Sales: Provide video link to sales team – ready for customer conversations
- Support: Use onboarding videos to reduce support requests

This isn't "also doing video." It's integrating video as the finishing touch on your systematic B2B content strategy.
