## Building Your Plan

**Step 1 (Week 1):** Interview sales (top 10 questions customers ask)

**Step 2 (Week 1):** Keyword research (30-minute sprint identifying 12–15 topics)

**Step 3 (Week 2):** Competitor analysis (what top 3 competitors rank for)

**Step 4 (Week 2):** Create editorial plan spreadsheet (topics, keywords, formats)

**Step 5 (Week 3–4):** Generate content in batches using AI

**Step 6 (Ongoing):** Publish on schedule, monitor, iterate

---

## Monthly Workflow (After Initial Plan)

**Beginning of month:**
- Review previous month's performance
- Add 4 new topics to backlog
- Brief AI platform with topics for next 4 weeks
- AI generates all content

**Mid-month:**
- Review generated content
- Make revisions
- Schedule distribution

**End of month:**
- Publish remaining content
- Analyze performance
- Plan next month

---

## Key Metrics to Track in Plan

- **Keyword rankings:** track position changes weekly
- **Traffic:** monthly organic visitors
- **Engagement:** average read time, scroll depth
- **Leads:** inquiries attributed to each article
- **AI citations:** how often your content appears in AI answers
