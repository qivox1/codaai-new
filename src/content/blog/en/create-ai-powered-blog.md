A blog used to require hiring a writer or paying an agency.

Today, it requires one person and an AI platform.

The result: 4 articles monthly, fully SEO-optimized, for less than one article costs at a traditional agency.

---

## The AI Blog Workflow

**Week 1:**
- List 4 topics your customers actually ask about
- Input into your AI platform
- AI generates 4 article drafts (3,000+ words each)

**Week 2:**
- Review for accuracy
- Add company-specific case studies
- Schedule distribution

**Week 3–4:**
- Publish on website and LinkedIn
- Monitor performance

**Total time: 15–20 hours/month**
